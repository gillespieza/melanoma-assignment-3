Cancer options
TL;DRWe evaluated ﬁve cancers against the ﬁve project questions (cross-dataset response prediction, cell viability modelling,ODE models, drug target discovery, and treatment type selection). The recommendation is Melanoma (SKCM), withNSCLC and TNBC as credible alternatives.
Recommended: Melanoma (SKCM)The BRAF V600E/K mutation splits patients into three clean treatment arms — dacarbazine (chemo), BRAF/MEKinhibitors (targeted), and PD-1/CTLA-4 checkpoint inhibitors — making it the strongest candidate for Q5. It also has awell-sized TCGA cohort (~473), mature ODE literature (Q3), deep DepMap coverage (~60 lines, Q2/Q4), and multiplepublished IO trial cohorts for cross-dataset validation (Q1). It scores well on all ﬁve questions.
AlternativesNSCLC (LUAD + LUSC, TCGA ~1,089) is the best choice if sample size is the priority. DepMap cell line coverage is the largestof any candidate (~150+ lines), though pooling two subtypes adds biological noise.
TNBC is the strongest breast cancer option, with a compelling chemo vs. pembrolizumab + chemo story (Q5) and highclinical urgency. The TCGA cohort is small (~170) and must be supplemented with METABRIC and trial datasets; budgetextra time for data harmonisation.
CRC (MSI-H subgroup) and ccRCC are viable but each carry a structural weakness — an extremely small immunotherapy-relevant cohort (~90–125 MSI-H patients) for CRC, and thin DepMap cell line coverage (~25–30 lines) for ccRCC.
RejectedGBM, pancreatic cancer, AML, HER2+ breast, ovarian cancer, and undiﬀerentiated all-subtype breast cancer were ruled outdue to small cohorts, weak immunotherapy stories, divergent biology, or limited DepMap representation.
Bottom LinePick Melanoma. If the group has a strong preference for breast cancer, pick TNBC — but plan for the extra datawrangling up front.

🥇  1. Melanoma (Skin Cutaneous Melanoma — SKCM)Recommended pick
Melanoma is the textbook precision oncology cancer. The BRAF V600E/K mutation cleanly splits patients into three treatmentarms, creating a natural framework for every project question.

Q2 ✅Strong~60 lines in DepMap/CCLE; vemurafenib, dabrafenib, trametinib, dacarbazine all in GDSC/PRISM

🥉  3. Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)Best breast cancer subgroup; strong clinical urgency
TNBC is the highest-unmet-need breast cancer subtype: ER−/PR−/HER2−, no targeted therapy historically, and only recentlygaining immunotherapy approvals. That makes the chemo-vs-immunotherapy question genuinely open and impactful.
