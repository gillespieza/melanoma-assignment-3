Interpretation:
  A log-rank p < 0.05 means the ODE readout separates melanoma patients by
  survival; the observed direction for each readout is stated above and is
  read directly from the data, not assumed.
   * Mechanistic pERK reflects MAPK-pathway output, which is greatest in
     NRAS-mutant tumours — the worst-prognosis SKCM subtype — so elevated
     pERK tracks more aggressive disease.
   * Modelled tumour burden (BRAFi sweep) integrates proliferation against
     immune control; high residual burden marks aggressive, immune-cold
     disease.
   * Modelled checkpoint tumour burden (anti-PD-1 sweep, Module D) marks
     disease that persists even under simulated checkpoint blockade —
     i.e. checkpoint-refractory tumours — expected to track worse OS just
     as the BRAFi-arm tumour burden does.
  All three readouts are prognostic where log-rank p < 0.05.
